PHYSICSUNITY

Unity · C# · Rigidbody · Raycasting

Custom Vehicle Physics & Character Controller

A Unity prototype combining a custom vehicle simulation, Rigidbody-based third-person movement and an orbit camera. Built without CharacterController, WheelCollider, Joint components or Cinemachine.

Technical breakdown
  • Raycast suspension with spring and damping forces.
  • Tyre friction, slip detection and handbrake slides.
  • Engine RPM, torque distribution and automatic/manual gear shifting.
  • Character air control, additional gravity and fall-over recovery.
  • Raycast interactions, vehicle entry/exit and camera target switching.

Development included resolving suspension jitter, reverse oversteer, invalid physics values and camera transitions. The prototype uses a simplified tyre friction model; camera collision handling remains limited.
